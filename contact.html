Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our preparation, packaging, shelf life, and Pan-India delivery.

How long does Pan-India delivery take? +

We deliver nationwide across India. Regular transit typically takes 3 to 6 business days depending on your pincode. We prioritize quick dispatches so your sweets arrive at peak freshness.

Are the sweets prepared fresh on order? +

Yes! Every single item is handcrafted in small daily batches using pure Desi Ghee, wholesome wheat flour, and natural sweeteners. We never use cold-storage pre-packaged items.

What is the shelf life of Thekua? +

Authentic Thekua has a natural shelf life of up to 30 days when kept in an airtight container away from direct sunlight and moisture. No refrigeration is required thanks to our traditional dry roasting technique.

Do you use artificial preservatives or food colors? +

Never. We adhere strictly to authentic family recipes: 100% natural spices like green cardamom and fennel, whole wheat, organic jaggery, pure desi ghee, and premium dry fruits.

Can I order custom corporate gift boxes? +

Absolutely. We specialize in festive and corporate gifting with bespoke branding ribbons, personalized greeting messages, and assorted sweet boxes. Reach out to us via email or call for bulk rates.

What is your damaged package replacement policy? +

Your satisfaction is our priority. In the rare event that your package is damaged during transit, simply share photos with our support team within 48 hours and we will ship an immediate replacement.

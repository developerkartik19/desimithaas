Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take? +

We provide delivery across Pan India. Delivery typically takes 3-7 business days depending on your location. Express delivery is available for selected areas.

Yes! All our products are prepared fresh daily using traditional methods. We use food-grade packaging to ensure freshness during transit.

We offer a 7-day return policy for damaged or unsatisfactory products. If you receive a damaged item, contact us immediately with photos, and we'll send a replacement.

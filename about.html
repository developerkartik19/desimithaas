About Desimithaas

Bringing Heritage to Your Doorstep

Our Story

Desimithaas was born from a passion for preserving traditional recipes and authentic flavors. What started as a family recipe has grown into a mission to deliver genuine, handcrafted sweets to every corner of India.

Our founder, inspired by grandmother's kitchen wisdom, decided to combine tradition with modern convenience. Every batch of Thekua is crafted with the same care, quality, and love that has been passed down through generations.

Today, we serve thousands of satisfied customers who trust us for authentic traditional sweets and a taste of heritage with every bite.
